NEON BOROUGH VR — Quest prototype v0.1.0

An original, small GTA-inspired city courier game. It is not Rockstar's GTA.
Designed for Meta Quest 2 / 3 / 3S with Touch controllers.

INSTALL
1. Enable Developer Mode for your Quest through Meta's developer setup.
2. Connect your Quest to a computer with a USB data cable.
3. In the headset, accept the USB debugging prompt.
4. Use SideQuest's "Install APK" action and choose Neon-Borough-VR.apk.
   If you already use Android platform tools, run:
   adb install -r Neon-Borough-VR.apk
5. Launch Neon Borough VR from your headset's Unknown Sources app list.

PLAY
A gold car is parked ahead. A cyan beacon marks the courier terminal nearby.
Walk to the terminal and press A to collect a package. Police are alerted.
Approach your gold car and press A to enter. Drive away from the police.
Keep both police cars more than 38 metres away for 9 seconds to clear heat.
Follow the cyan destination beacon and the HUD's direction and distance.
Stop, press A to exit, then press A near the beacon to deliver your package.
Return to the terminal for another contract. Cash and completed jobs save locally.

TOUCH CONTROLLERS
Left stick: walk in the direction you look; steer/throttle/reverse when driving.
Right trigger: accelerate when driving.
Right grip: brake.
Right stick: 30-degree snap turns on foot.
Right A or left X: collect, deliver, enter car, exit stopped car.
Right B: return to the hub and cancel the active job; banked cash is preserved.

Desktop fallback in the source project: WASD, mouse, E interact, Space brake,
R return to hub, Escape release mouse.

SCOPE AND TESTING
This is a compact prototype: a procedural city, one usable gold car, traffic,
two pursuing police cars, four rotating delivery locations, collision damage,
HUD guidance, generated sound effects, and local progress saving.
It does not include GTA's map, story, multiplayer, combat, or Rockstar assets.
Mission logic and desktop OpenGL rendering were tested in the build environment.
APK signature, ARM64 libraries, VR launch categories and OpenXR manifest were
checked. Installation, tracking, controller input, sound, comfort and performance
have not been tested on a physical Quest. Use current Quest system software.

APK package: org.neonborough.vr
Architecture: arm64-v8a; Android minimum API 29; target API 32.
Signed with a local prototype certificate for sideloading; no store publication.
No network account or game Internet connection is required.

The engine is Godot 4.6.3 (MIT), with the Khronos OpenXR Android loader 1.1.54
(Apache-2.0 OR MIT). Third-party license notices are embedded in the APK.
